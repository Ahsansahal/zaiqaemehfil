“The taste of a diverse gathering”

Built around our community

Before we opened our doors on Greenford Road, we spent time getting to know the neighbourhood — the families, the regulars and the friends who meet after work — and what they really love to eat.

Harrow is one of London’s most diverse boroughs, and the answer came back rich and varied. Pakistani households wanted nihari and charsi karahi made the traditional way. Afghan families wanted a proper Kabuli pulao and a crisp chapli kebab. And nearly every table wanted something Indo-Chinese, like Chicken Manchurian, to share.

So we stopped asking which cuisine to cook, and brought all three together under one golden roof — each dish cooked the way it’s meant to be, and served to share.

That’s the meaning behind our name. Zaiqa is taste. Mehfil is a gathering. Zaiqa-e-Mehfil is the taste of a diverse gathering: a place where every family and every friend finds their favourite on the same table.
